How to play

You wake in a bed, and the power is out. You have three days. Every time he catches you, you wake up on the next day in the same house, and he gets faster. Get caught on day three and he drags you away for good. The house has 3 or 4 floors joined by two rusted steel stairwells built onto the outside of the house, behind green-lit fire doors in the east and west walls. You can really climb them, and he uses them too. Watch the floor for rusty bear traps: they hold you still and the snap is loud. The exit on the ground floor is sealed: find 4 fuses: they sit on pedestals, hide deep inside vent tunnels, or just lie around loose, then open the exit and walk into the light.

Close doors behind you. A shut door hides you from his eyes, and an open one you did not open means he has already been through.

Your flashlight runs on batteries. Take bottles and throw one: he will go and look. You can only carry one item at a time. Pick it up with E and drop it with SPACE to free your hands. A brass key opens the room he never visits; spare batteries are used with R. Press C or CTRL to toggle crouch: you move slowly, but quietly and lower. Vents are real tunnels inside the thick walls. Each cover is held by 4 screws: carry a screwdriver to one, aim at a screw and press E; each screw drops out at once, and the last one lets the cover fall open with a clang. It stays open. From inside a vent you can push the cover out with E, no screwdriver needed. Crouch (C) and simply walk in. The tunnels join other rooms and halls, and a fuse sometimes hides deep inside. While you are in the walls he cannot see you or catch you, even if he watched you climb in. Anything that makes noise (dropping or throwing things, unscrewing a vent) reaches him no matter how far away he is. Notes tell you what happened here.

The halls are lit up by default, with no fog. Switch on fog and darkness in Options for the scary version. From Extreme up they are always on, for everyone in the party.

Lockers are your only shelter. If he sees you climb in, he will drag you out. Sprinting is loud. Lights fail. Don't stop moving.

TOUCH: left thumb drag = move  ·  right thumb drag = look  ·  USE = E (open, take, hide, unscrew vents)  ·  SPRINT = tap to toggle (stops when out of breath)  ·  LIGHT = flashlight  ·  THROW = G  ·  DROP = SPACE  ·  BATT = R  ·  CROUCH = C  ·  II = pause
